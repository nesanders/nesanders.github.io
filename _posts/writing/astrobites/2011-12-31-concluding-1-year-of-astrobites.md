*This post originally appeared on Astrobites.org on December 31, 2011 at [https://astrobites.org/2011/12/31/concluding-1-year-of-astrobites/](https://astrobites.org/2011/12/31/concluding-1-year-of-astrobites/).*

We made our first post in late December of 2010, so we'd like to take a look back at what Astrobites has become over its first year. Do you have a favorite Astrobite that has stuck in your mind? Please let us know by leaving a comment below.

[![](https://astrobites.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/astrocake.JPG)](https://astrobites.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/astrocake.JPG)

*A birthday cake for Astrobites.*

Astrobites was founded with one goal: to help undergraduates participate in research by introducing them to the astrophysical literature. But we've also tried to address broader needs in the astronomical community. We've [opened up our site to undergraduates](https://astrobites.com/category/undergraduate-research/) to share their own research (you can [submit your research abstract here](https://astrobites.com/about/undergraduate-research-abstract-submission/)). We've collected [our best advice for navigating every stage of graduate school](https://astrobites.com/glossaries/astrobites-graduate-school-guide/) as part of our [Astrobites glossaries](https://astrobites.com/glossaries/). We even visited the [AAS meeting in Boston](https://astrobites.com/2011/05/22/visit-astrobites-at-aas-219-in-boston/) this summer, distributing free cookies to any astronomer who stopped by (this was perhaps the greatest need in the community at the time).

We've summarized [about 250](https://astrobites.com/category/daily-paper-summaries/) research papers on everything from [exoplanets](https://astrobites.com/tag/exoplanets/) to [galaxies](https://astrobites.com/tag/galaxies/). We've racked up nearly 200,000 page views and grown from just five contributors at Harvard University to [27 graduate students from around the world](https://astrobites.com/meet-the-authors/). We are grateful for all the support we've received along the way; especially to everyone who has contributed to Astrobites, all the readers who have given us their feedback, for generous introductions from [Phil Plait](https://blogs.discovermagazine.com/badastronomy/2011/01/15/astrobites/) and [Astrobetter](https://www.astrobetter.com/introducing-astrobites/), and for hosting from [VoxCharta](https://voxcharta.org/).
